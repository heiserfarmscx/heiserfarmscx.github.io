The course

Fast, technical, and always fun.

Get ready for a 1.9-mile cyclocross battle at Heiser Farms, built for speed, bike handling, and tactical choices. Grass, fast gravel, and rolling terrain lead into fun, technical forest sections.

A medium-length run-up about halfway through the course leads to more forest riding, followed by the infamous Bog. A final climb takes you back to the start/finish.
